## Assumption/Vacuity Triage Improvement Chain

The v1.1.2 baseline exposed a systematic issue: only 3 of 12 gold `assumption_constraint_bug` cases were classified correctly. Several missed cases had explanations that mentioned assumptions or overconstraint, but the final label/action still selected `assertion_property_bug` and `fix_assertion_property`.

The v1.1.5 change added structured, non-gold assumption/vacuity cues to the evidence path and prompt:

- blocking assumption cues;
- reset-stuck assumption cues;
- missing environment constraint cues;
- vacuous or overconstrained property cues;
- normalization that keeps any issue/action adjustment visible in `debug_checklist`.

The scoped real local Qwen gate then reached 12/12 on gold `assumption_constraint_bug` cases:

| Run | Scope | Cases | Issue/action |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| v1.1.2 baseline | Full local Qwen triage | 12 assumption/constraint cases | 3/12 |
| v1.1.5 scoped gate | Real local Qwen assumption/vacuity gate | 12 assumption/constraint cases | 12/12 |
| v1.1.6 full rerun | Full local Qwen triage | 53 total cases | 0.962 / 0.962 overall; 12/12 assumption/constraint |

This supports a focused claim: structured assumption/vacuity evidence improved local Qwen triage behavior on the observed weak class and transferred to the full 53-case triage setting.

It does not prove that all assumption or vacuity problems in arbitrary RTL will be solved, and it is not JasperGold-backed triage validation.

## Stimulus-vs-Coverage Improvement Chain

The v1.1.6 full rerun fixed assumption/vacuity but exposed a second boundary issue: the model confused missing or insufficient stimulus with reachable coverage closure.

Two cases defined the observed failure mode:

| Case | Gold issue/action | v1.1.6 prediction | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rv_B8` | `testbench_stimulus_bug` / `fix_testbench_or_stimulus` | `reachable_coverage_gap` / `add_directed_test_or_sequence` | Regressed from the v1.1.2 prediction; missing dequeue stimulus was treated as coverage closure. |
| `fifo_D17` | `testbench_stimulus_bug` / `fix_testbench_or_stimulus` | `reachable_coverage_gap` / `add_directed_test_or_sequence` | Persistent miss; missing `pop_ready` stimulus was treated as a directed coverage opportunity. |

The v1.1.7 scoped change added a derived `stimulus_context` and a conservative stimulus-vs-coverage decision rule:

- missing or insufficient stimulus maps to `testbench_stimulus_bug` / `fix_testbench_or_stimulus`;
- reachable cover with valid environment and witness-like support maps to `reachable_coverage_gap` / `add_directed_test_or_sequence`;
- illegal, invalid, or unreachable goals map to `unreachable_or_invalid_coverage_goal`;
- an unhit cover goal alone is not enough to claim a reachable coverage gap.

The scoped real local Qwen gate covered all 18 stimulus, reachable-coverage, and invalid/unreachable guardrail cases. It reached:

| Stimulus-vs-coverage scoped gate | 18 | 1.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 18/18 |

## Why This Is Research-Relevant

The post-v1.1.3 triage work is stronger than a one-shot prompt tweak because it follows an auditable research loop:

1. The full local Qwen run exposed a specific failure mode.
2. The fix added structured evidence cues tied to that failure mode.
3. A scoped real-model gate checked the targeted behavior first.
4. A full local Qwen rerun checked transfer and non-target regressions.
5. Raw model outputs remained ignored; curated summaries recorded metrics and boundaries.

This preserves the JasperLoop-DV thesis: the LLM is not the verification oracle. The system improves reliability by constraining model outputs with schemas, issue taxonomies, signal validation, evidence packets, fallback accounting, hallucinated-signal measurement, and formal-tool boundaries where JasperGold is actually run.

It also separates evidence sources:

- deterministic scaffold and replay checks are not reported as real model performance;
- local Qwen results are reported as local real-LLM results through `JASPERLOOP_LLM_CMD`;
- JasperGold-backed results are reported only where JasperGold actually re-checked saved SVA candidates;
- no Codex CLI, official FVEval, or production signoff claims are made.

## Remaining Limitations

- The v1.1.8 result is not JasperGold-backed triage validation. Triage labels are still benchmark labels and structured evidence outcomes, not formal proof obligations.
- The benchmark size remains modest: 53 local failure-triage cases.
- The real-model result is for local Qwen/Qwen3-14B-AWQ only. It is not Codex CLI performance and not evidence that another model will behave the same way.
- SVA repair and coverage closure were not rerun after the triage-specific changes.
- The saved SVA repair JasperGold re-check remains scoped to SVA repair final candidates from v1.1.2, not to triage or coverage recommendations.
- Official FVEval reproduction has not been completed.
- JasperGold proof, where available, is scoped to the harnesses, assumptions, and checked properties; it is not full semantic intent equivalence.
- The current triage improvements may be partly due to the combined evidence, prompt, and normalization pipeline. A future ablation is needed to separate these contributions.
